# Third-Party Audio Notices

## ODAQ training excerpt: `assets/odaq-26-claves-reference.wav`

This project includes the ODAQ training sample `26_claves/reference.wav`, provided here as a 10-second, 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM WAV file. The source material is described by ODAQ as a solo wooden claves recording by Samuel Gremaud, originally published on Freesound as [517609](https://freesound.org/people/SamuelGremaud/sounds/517609/). ODAQ lists this source under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Its training copy has been cut, repeated, scaled, and processed by the ODAQ authors.

ODAQ is a research dataset created by Matteo Torcoli, Chih-Wei Wu, Sascha Dick, Phillip A. Williams, Mhd Modar Halimeh, William Wolcott, and Emanuël A. P. Habets. Dataset details, materials, and license disclaimer:

- [ODAQ dataset on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10405774)
- [ODAQ project and detailed license information](https://github.com/Fraunhofer-IIS/ODAQ)

Suggested citation:

> Matteo Torcoli, Chih-Wei Wu, Sascha Dick, Phillip A. Williams, Mhd Modar Halimeh, William Wolcott, and Emanuël A. P. Habets, “ODAQ: Open Dataset of Audio Quality,” IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 2024.

This sample is an open listening stimulus from an expert-listener audio-quality dataset. It is not a Harman recording, and its presence does not imply that it is part of Harman “How to Listen” or that it is an official Harman standard test track.

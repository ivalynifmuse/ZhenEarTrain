臻耳朵训练室 · 用户手册

英文名：JANEAR Listening Lab。“JANEAR”是呼应“臻耳”读音的自拟拼写；“Near”也表达贴近声音、细致聆听。它是本项目自拟名称，不是 Harman 产品名称。

开始训练

  1. 使用耳机并选择安静环境。先把系统音量调低，再缓慢调至舒适水平；本网页不测量或校准声压。
  2. 在“训练任务”选择练习（Practice，即时反馈）或测试（Session，结束后反馈）、任务、必要的任务选项、素材和题数。
  3. 依次播放 A（参考声）和 B（任务变化声），可重复试听，再提交答案。左右切换只切换同一试次的版本。
  4. 记录只存于当前浏览器。更换设备或清理站点数据不会同步记录。

训练项目

  • 频段辨识：辨认峰值/凹陷或低通/高通变化所在频率。此项目中 B 是均衡/滤波处理声。
  • 声音属性：判断明亮度、丰满度、染色、混响、噪声、哼声或左右平衡的变化。每种属性由对应处理生成。
  • 频谱轮廓：听辨低频/高频提升及中频峰值/凹陷类型。
  • 左右声像定位：逐个反复试听候选声；播放器横轴上的 A–N 小图标可点击试听或停止，也可拖动排序。
  • 偏好评分：按 1–5 记录个人偏好；没有客观正确答案，不计入正确率。

训练指标与测试等级

  • 正确率：客观任务按试次计分，偏好题除外。空间任务让被试反复试听多个候选声，并在播放器横轴上拖动 A–N 图标，按左到右排序；每题按候选两两之间的顺序准确率计分，达到 80% 记为该题通过，Session 得分为所有两两比较准确率的平均值。Level 1 排 3 个候选，每升一级加 1 个，最高 Level 12 排 14 个。
  • 连续正确、累计答题、最近得分：描述当前训练记录，不是听力诊断。
  • 难度 Level 1–12：本工具内部的参数刻度，不是 Harman 等级、ITU/ISO 认证或听力能力等级。自动模式按一轮表现调整：≥80% 上调一级，<50% 下调一级，其余不变；只影响后续试题参数。
  • 正式测试：可多选客观训练项；每个项目各自从 Level 1 开始，每级 5 题，答对至少 4 题晋级，未达到则结束该项，最高 Level 12。空间排序题需单题两两顺序准确率达到 80% 才算答对。偏好没有客观正确答案，不纳入正式测试。测试规则为 JANEAR 自定义，不是 Harman 等级或认证。结果仅保存在本浏览器。
  • 练习 Session：隐藏单题即时答案，结束后显示本轮结果。建议按任务分别查看多轮结果；设备、耳机、音量和听音环境都会影响分数。

空间任务的测量边界

页面用 Web Audio StereoPannerNode 生成双声道左右声像，并把候选声均匀放在归一化 pan（−0.9…+0.9）范围内。候选位置在左右声像范围内等距排列；被试只判断相对次序，不必估计精确坐标。Level 1 排序 3 个候选，每级加 1 个，Level 10 排序 12 个。这是 JANEAR 自定义进阶表，并非 Harman 已公开的等级规则；心理声学研究显示候选声源数量增加、声源间距缩小时定位判断准确率会下降，但没有规定本工具的级数或数量。正态分布描述观测值的统计分散，不是选择训练档位数量的标准；本工具固定等距候选以保持各题难度更可比。pan 值不是角度；耳机、佩戴、耳廓、头动和设备串音都会影响感知。本版本不测前后、垂直、距离、外化或真实声场定位，也不使用个体化 HRTF。

术语与标准依据

频率（Hz）：每秒周期数。频带：由上下限频率界定的连续范围。低频、中频、高频在听音训练语境中没有适用于所有标准、设备与任务的统一固定分界；因此请以题目给出的 Hz、中心频率及滤波器类型为准，不把这些标签当作绝对分界。ISO 266 规定优选频率系列；IEC 61260-1 规定倍频程和分数倍频程滤波器。具体边界须由任务声明。

声压级（dB SPL）：声压相对于空气中 20 μPa 参考声压的对数表示；网页没有声压校准，所以无法给出可信 SPL。响度：主观听觉量，受声级、频率、波形、带宽和时长等影响；ISO 532-2 的响度单位为 sone。不得把本页 EQ 增益 dB 当作听力阈值或“能听出几分贝”。

EQ（均衡）：改变频率响应的滤波处理。Flat（平直）：在频响语境里指相对于指定测量链/参考条件的响应；不等于一段录音本身绝对平直。本工具统一称 A 为参考声、B 为任务变化声；仅频段辨识中的变化是 EQ/滤波。

空间定位：对声源方向/位置的感知。ITU-R BS.1116 将定位质量与空间/环境质量作为不同描述维度。ITU-T P.811 针对耳机语音立体声系统分别评定信号质量、空间定位和整体质量。这些标准不为本工具定义 Level 或通过线。

标准与资料

  • Giguère & Abel：比较 3、5、7 个声源条件下的定位正确率与响应时长
  • ITU-R BS.1116-3：小型音频劣化主观评价方法（含定位质量等空间描述）。
  • ITU-T P.811：耳机语音立体声系统主观评价。
  • ISO 532-2：响度计算方法与相关术语。
  • ISO 266：优选频率。
  • IEC 61260-1：倍频程及分数倍频程滤波器。

JANEAR Listening Lab · User Manual

Name: “JANEAR” is a coined spelling chosen to echo the sound of 臻耳; “Near” also suggests listening closely. This is a project-coined name, not a Harman product name.

Getting started

  1. Use headphones in a quiet room. Start with a low system volume and raise it slowly to a comfortable level. This app does not measure or calibrate sound pressure.
  2. In Training, choose Practice (immediate feedback) or Session (feedback at the end), then choose a task, its relevant option, material, and trial count.
  3. Play A (reference) and B (task-modified) for the same trial. Replay as needed, then submit.
  4. History stays in this browser and does not sync across devices.

Exercises

  • Band identification: identify the frequency region and type of peak/dip or low-/high-pass change. B is equalized/filtered for this task.
  • Sound attributes: judge changes in brightness, fullness, coloration, reverberation, noise, hum, or left/right balance.
  • Spectral profile: identify bass/treble boost or midrange peak/dip.
  • Horizontal image localization: replay each candidate and drag candidates onto the horizontal axis in perceived left-to-right order; each candidate toggles play/stop.
  • Preference: record a personal 1–5 preference. There is no objectively correct answer and it is excluded from accuracy.

Metrics and test levels

  • Accuracy: objective tasks are scored by trial, excluding preference. For spatial trials, listeners replay several candidates and drag them onto a horizontal axis in left-to-right order. Each trial is scored by pairwise ordering accuracy; 80% passes a trial, and the Session score is the mean pairwise accuracy across trials. Level 1 has 3 candidates; each higher level adds one, up to 14 at Level 12.
  • Level 1–12: an internal task-parameter scale, not a Harman, ITU, or ISO grade or certification. In auto mode, a completed round at ≥80% raises one level; below 50% lowers one; other scores hold. It changes later trial parameters.
  • Formal test: select one or more objectively scored tasks. Each starts at Level 1; each level has 5 trials and requires at least 4 correct to advance. A failed level ends that task; the maximum is Level 12. Spatial ranking trials count as correct when at least 80% of candidate pairs are ordered correctly. Preference has no objective answer and is excluded. These are JANEAR-defined test rules, not Harman grades or certification. Results stay in this browser.
  • Practice Session: hides immediate answers and shows the round result at completion. There is no official pass threshold or professional grade.

Spatial measurement limits

This page uses Web Audio StereoPannerNode to create a two-channel left/right image, then places candidates at evenly spaced points across normalized pan −0.9…+0.9. Candidates are equally spaced across the left-to-right image; listeners judge relative order rather than estimate exact coordinates. Level 1 ranks 3 candidates and each level adds one, up to 14 at Level 12. This is JANEAR’s own progression, not a published Harman level table. Psychophysical research finds localization accuracy decreases as source count increases and source spacing shrinks, but does not prescribe this app’s levels or counts. A normal distribution describes variation in observations; it is not a standard for choosing level counts. Fixed equal spacing keeps trial difficulty more comparable. Pan values are not angles. Headphones, ears, fit, head movement, and device crosstalk affect perception. This version does not measure front/back, elevation, distance, externalization, or individualized HRTF localization.

Terms and standards

Frequency (Hz) is cycles per second. A frequency band is a continuous range bounded by specified frequencies. “Low”, “mid”, and “high” do not have one universal fixed boundary across listening tasks and standards; use the stated Hz, center frequency, and filter type. ISO 266 specifies preferred frequencies; IEC 61260-1 specifies octave and fractional-octave filters. A task must state its own limits.

Sound-pressure level (dB SPL) is a logarithmic level referenced to 20 μPa in air. This app is not SPL-calibrated. Loudness is perceptual and depends on level, frequency, waveform, bandwidth, and duration; ISO 532-2 expresses loudness in sones. EQ gain in dB is not a hearing threshold or a “how many dB can you hear” rating.

Equalization (EQ) changes frequency response. Flat refers to response relative to a specified measurement chain and reference conditions; it does not mean a recording is perfectly flat. The app labels A as Reference and B as Task change; only band identification uses EQ/filter processing.

Spatial localization is perception of source direction or position. ITU-R BS.1116 treats localization quality separately from spatial/environment quality. ITU-T P.811 separately rates signal quality, spatial localization, and overall quality for headphone speech stereo. Neither standard defines this app’s levels or pass thresholds.
